# LAB-REPORT-3
OBJECTIVES:
The primary objective of this laboratory is to conduct a study of Amplitude Shift Keying (ASK), Frequency Shift Keying (FSK), and Binary Phase Shift Keying (BPSK). In this lab, we will first explore Amplitude Shift Keying (ASK), Frequency Shift Keying (FSK), and Binary Phase Shift Keying (BPSK) to observe how digital data is encoded onto an analog carrier's amplitude, frequency, or phase. Additionally, this laboratory is designed to investigate high-speed data transfer to understand the trade offs between transmission rates and the resulting bandwidth occupancy. Another core objective is to conduct Quadrature Phase Shift Keying (QPSK) to explore spectral efficiency by splitting a single data stream into two independent channels that modulate orthogonal carriers. On the other side, we will implement Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS) to observe how spreading a message's power over a wide frequency range provides robustness against interference and enhances communication security. Finally, the last objective of this laboratory is to demonstrate the principles of Undersampling in Software Defined Radio (SDR). This involves using a sample-and-hold scheme at rates below the carrier frequency to perform direct down-conversion.
